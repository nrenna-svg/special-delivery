# Special Delivery — v0.76

Around the world. Right on time.

A separate tiny-planet delivery prototype; the original 2D BI-PLANE BATTLE is unchanged. Static HTML/JS, no build step. Serve dist/ over HTTP. Three.js 0.180.0 is vendored with its MIT license.

## Career mode (v0.70, the game now)
The game starts inside the Kestrel Courier Co. hangar on the home island. A corkboard job board offers four jobs; pick one and take it. The hangar doors slide open, the plane taxis out, turns onto the long runway and takes off (Space skips). Fly the job, come home and land: either touch down on the runway yourself for a cash bonus, or fly through the gold ring off the runway's +z end for an automatic landing with no bonus. The plane is towed back into the hangar and a pay ledger shows what you earned.
- Job types (career.js `JOB_TYPES`): parcel run (2-4 drops), crate haul (hook a crate off a tower with the hook line, then drop it on its pad), mail pickup (snag mailbags off masts), urgent medicine (one drop against the clock), boat supply (one drop on a moving boat plus one on the dock).
- Island danger tiers (career.js `TIERS`, `ISLAND_TIER`): CALM (Homeward Isles, Emerald Reach, Western Reach) ×1 pay, a crash costs repairs; BREEZY (Eastern Wilds, Lighthouse Coast) ×1.7, wind and gulls; ROUGH (Coral Keys, Southern Atolls, Orchid Isle) ×2.6, storms and waterspouts, a crash fails the job and the client pays nothing; DEADLY (Puffin Rocks, Sunset Archipelago) ×4, the worst weather, and a crash out there ends the whole career run. Pay also scales with distance.
- Pay: each objective pays an equal share of the job, scaled by drop accuracy and cargo condition (urgent on time ×1.4). Finishing everything adds 15%. A manual landing adds 15-70% on top, graded GREASED IT / FIRM / BOUNCY, +10% if centered. Repairs are deducted per crash. Cash, jobs done and career earnings are saved in localStorage (`special-delivery-career`, best career in `-best`).
- Manual landing (airfield.js `gradeTouchdown`): touch down on the runway with sink ≤ 26 m/s, speed ≤ 72 kt, wings level and lined up with the runway (either direction). Below 48 kt the plane settles on its own (flight.js `SLOW_SPEED`/`SLOW_SINK`), so the easy way down is: line up, throttle back, let it sink. An approach readout (speed, sink, line-up) appears near the runway. After touchdown the plane brakes to a stop; overrunning the end is a crash.
- Missed or smashed crates (v0.72): on CALM and BREEZY islands a crate that misses its pad stays where it landed (floating on water) with its ring up, so you can hook it again (−15% cargo condition, wider 3.4 hook radius since it sits low); a smashed crate is restacked on its tower (−30%). On ROUGH and DEADLY islands you get one shot: a lost crate fails the job. A failed job, or a parcel job that runs out of parcels, always shows the gold ring and points you home.
- Calling off a flight (the footer button) tows you home with no pay.
- Endless Run and Training Days are hidden, not deleted: set `LEGACY_MODES=true` in game.js to bring the old menus back (they have not been re-checked since the world and runway changed).
- World: planet radius 270 (was 200; grown 35% again). The home island is bigger (size 62) to fit a 66-unit runway that follows the planet's curve, an apron and the quonset hangar (hangar.js builds the scenery; airfield.js has the runway frame, rollout path and landing rules).
- Automated tests can read live state through `window.__sd` (read-only hooks).

## Plane shop, ranks and worlds (v0.74)
- **Plane shop** (hangar tab, upgrades.js): 8 parts. Each must be unlocked first (v0.76: every part, including Engine $600, Fuel tank $500 and Cargo hold $400, so each part is 6 purchases) (one-time fee, gated by pilot rank), then upgraded through 5 levels; each level costs about 2× the last and improves the part only a little (≈20-25% when maxed). Engine (speed, acceleration), Fuel tank (+10% flying time per level), Cargo hold (+1 spare parcel), Wings (climb, ceiling, slower stall), Ailerons (turn rate, bank response, roll speed), Parachutes (open sooner, drift less, softer landing), Winch & hook (longer, faster line, wider hook), Landing gear & brakes (harder/faster touchdowns, stronger brakes). Effects flow through `state.perf` (flight.js), `fuelBurn(..,perf)`, chute options in delivery.js, `gradeTouchdown(..,lim)`, and the winch in game.js. Every level of every part visibly changes the plane (game.js `LOOKS`: e.g. engine cowl → chrome exhausts → radial cylinder heads → chrome spinner → gold spinner and scoop; gear: fat tyres → spats → struts → tail wheel → gold hubs). Buying plays a workshop sequence (audio.js `workshop`: impact wrench, hammers, drill, ratchet) with sparks while the new pieces pop into place. In the hangar the plane is parked deeper and the camera frames it to the left of the job board (bottom on phones).
- **Pilot ranks** (upgrades.js `RANKS`): Rookie → Courier (3 jobs, $2.5k earned) → Senior Courier (10, $15k) → Ace (25, $60k) → Legend (50, $200k). Wings/Ailerons/Parachutes unlock at Courier ($3k each); Winch and Landing gear at Senior Courier ($10k each).
- **Worlds** (worlds.js): Kestrel (start) and **Mesa Rojo**, a desert canyon world: red-rock mesas of different heights rising from a sand sea, buttes beside the tall mesas forming canyons, hoodoos (the sea stacks) and dust devils (the waterspouts) on the open sand, sandstorms (storm cells), vultures, cacti and adobe houses. Delivery sites sit on the mesa tops (`groundHeight` in hazards.js; parcels and crates land on top). No boat jobs there; every job pays 1.8×. Unlock: Senior Courier rank, Engine and Fuel tank level 3, and a $100,000 ferry license. Switching worlds plays a short ferry card and reloads the page (`special-delivery-world` in localStorage). Each world can see the other hanging in its sky. A career over (DEADLY crash) resets everything, including parts and worlds.

## Controls
- Mouse: steer. The cursor's offset from screen center banks left/right and climbs/dives (mouse up = nose up); a dashed ring marks the dead zone and a crosshair follows the cursor. Steering starts on the first mouse move after takeoff, resume or landing.
- W / S (or minus / plus, or the slider): throttle; Shift: boost
- Mouse wheel: winch the hook line down (scroll down) or up (scroll up), 14 m per notch, up to 140 m; it never hangs lower than just above the ground and reels in automatically for landing
- Q / E: left / right barrel roll; F (or L): loop (minimum 160m and 51 knots)
- Space: drop cargo; cargo retains flight velocity
- Arrow keys: steer without a mouse
- P / Escape: pause; R: restart route (replays the opening cutscene)
- Cutscene: Space, Enter, Escape or the SKIP button jumps straight to the hand-over
- Touch: steering pad and on-screen action buttons

Deliver to five numbered pads on Kestrel Island, in any order. The gold ring predicts cargo impact. Pads accept drops within 8 world units. Fly through the blue depot hoop to refill 12 parcels and restore cargo condition. Stunts slightly reduce fragile cargo condition. Score reflects landing accuracy, cargo condition, and a route completion time bonus. Misses use a parcel; refill or restart to try again. Personal best is stored only in this browser.

Run `node --test tests/*.test.mjs`. Tests cover spherical flight, altitude constraints, stunt recovery, throttle, cargo momentum/prediction, duplicate scoring, and route completion.

Crashes are forgiving (see Fuel and crashes below). The camera stays oriented to the planet during stunts. The existing hosting URL is retained.

Parachute delivery update: 0.65s release delay, 1s free fall, 0.45s canopy inflation. Low or hard impacts destroy cargo even inside a delivery pad. The altitude marker adds a safety margin for the current descent rate and release delay; maneuvering after pressing Drop changes the actual release trajectory. Parachute strips, cords, opening animation, scattered crate debris and LOST GOODS feedback are rendered in 3D. Landing prediction uses the same parachute simulation.

Live mission map: heading-up circular Route view with numbered delivery pads, completed markers, nearest-target highlight, blue depot and off-map direction arrows. World toggle shows the full globe using an azimuthal projection. Land outlines come from the scene geometry; objectives come from the current route every update, so completion and resets are reflected automatically.

Opening cutscene (v0.14): every mission (Start, Replay, Restart) opens on Kestrel Field. Close-up of two loaders carrying parcels from a baggage cart to the plane's open cargo door while a marshal checks a clipboard (5.2s), then the camera sweeps back to the chase position as the door closes and the crew waves (1.6s), then the takeoff roll and climb-out (5.1s), handing control to the player level at 150m and 54 knots. Letterbox bars, captions and SKIP button; the flight HUD hides until hand-over; route timer starts at hand-over. Timing and the takeoff path live in dist/takeoff.js (pure, tested in tests/takeoff.test.mjs); the crew, cart and apron in dist/crew.js; the cutscene camera and mode handling in dist/game.js (search "cutscene"). The blue depot hoop is hidden during the close-up only.

Delivery celebrations (v0.20): each stop receives named goods (shown in the dispatch panel). A successful drop leaves the crate on the pad with its parachute settling; a recipient in the stop's color runs out, picks it up, hops with it overhead, waves and heads home, with confetti on touchdown and pickup (dist/celebrate.js, people built with dist/people.js, shared with the Kestrel Field crew). Because the plane has flown on by then, a LIVE picture-in-picture window (#pip, rendered by renderPip in game.js into that element's rectangle) shows the doorstep for 5.4s. A postmark stamp in the stop's color shows goods, stop, points and accuracy. The landing preview ring turns green and grows when the drop will land on an open pad (padAt in delivery.js).

Sound (v0.20): dist/audio.js synthesizes everything with Web Audio: engine (saw + sub-octave with propeller tremolo, pitch from airspeed), wind that rises with speed and dives, effects for release, parachute, delivery, stamp, miss, smash, refill, stunts, touchdown, cheering and route complete, and a 16-bar 108 BPM ukulele/marimba loop (musicEvents is pure and tested). SOUND and MUSIC toggles are remembered in localStorage. Audio starts on the first click (browser autoplay rules).

Homecoming landing (v0.20): after the fifth delivery a gold gate (with three glide-path rings) appears over the sea off the runway's +Z end, marked H on the mission map. Fly through it heading toward the field to start a scripted glide, flare, touchdown and rollout (dist/landing.js). Speed through the gate grades the landing: 51 knots or less SMOOTH +150, up to 63 FIRM +80, faster BOUNCY +30 (with a bounce); within 1.5 units of the centerline +50. The crew cheers, the camera circles the parked plane, then the route-complete card shows the landing grade. The blue depot hoop moved beside the runway (normal -0.08,1,0.06) so takeoff and landing paths are clear.

Delivery days (v0.22): five routes defined in dist/days.js. Day 1 Kestrel Morning (the original five Kestrel stops), Day 2 The Emerald Run, Day 3 Atoll Hopping (Southern Atolls + Western Reach), Day 4 Coast to Coast (Lighthouse Coast, Homeward Isles, Eastern Wilds; 6 stops), Day 5 Around the World (one stop on each far island; 7 stops). Every day opens with the loading cutscene and ends with the landing at Kestrel Field. Stops on other islands are offsets in that island's surface frame (islandPoint); tests/days.test.mjs checks every pad is on land, pads don't overlap and every route completes. Trees and houses are kept off all pads. A day picker on the title and route-complete cards shows each day's best; finishing a day unlocks the next and preselects it. Progress is stored per browser (special-delivery-unlocked, special-delivery-best for day 1, special-delivery-best-dN). Each day has its own par time for the time bonus (300/360/420/480/600 s).

Theme music (v0.22): replaced the oom-pah tune with a 92 BPM adventure theme in D major: horn melody with vibrato, string pad chords, harp-like eighth-note arpeggio, bass and timpani with rolls into each half. createAudio({context}) accepts an OfflineAudioContext and queueMusic(seconds) for rendering previews.

Day challenges (v0.38): each day adds something new, and Day 5 combines them. Rules are pure functions in dist/challenges.js (tested in tests/challenges.test.mjs); visuals, per-frame updates and events are in dist/extras.js; layouts per day come from dayFeatures() in dist/days.js.
- Day 1 sky rings: gold rings along the route (ringCourse); 25 points each, streak multiplier up to x4 when rings are taken within 9 s of each other.
- Day 2 wind: a steady breeze with gusts (windAt). Parachuted parcels drift downwind (stepParcel relaxes canopy drift toward the wind); the landing preview includes the wind. Windsocks on every pad, drifting wind streaks near the plane, and a wind arrow relative to your heading in the dispatch panel.
- Day 3 moving targets: the ferry Gull and fishing boat Marlin sail circles at sea with their pads on deck (boatNormal); the green ring checks where the boat will be when the parcel lands. Urgent medicine for the turtle rescue: 95 s clock, +150 rush bonus if on time, red ! badge, ticking in the last 10 s.
- Day 4 mailbags and gulls: three striped masts with a bag hanging from a crossbar at 94 m; lower the hook line with the mouse wheel and sweep the hook within about 26 m of the bag to snag it (+100 each). All mailbags are required before the landing gate appears. Gull flocks circle on the long legs; hitting one costs 12% cargo condition.
- Day 5 storms and nightfall: three storm cells on alternate long legs (rain, lightning with thunder, gloom, turbulence that shakes the controls and wears cargo condition); gull flocks on the other legs; rings, lighter wind and an urgent birthday cake (7 min). The sky goes from afternoon to sunset to night over the day (skyAt); stars appear, houses and pads glow and the plane carries a lamp.

Fuel and crashes (v0.46, forgiving rules; make harsher later if needed): the tank burns 0.22 %/s plus 0.42 x throttle, and boost adds 1.9 %/s (fuelBurn in flight.js); a full tank lasts about four minutes at cruise. Refuel at the blue depot at home (now parcels and fuel) or through green F fuel hoops over far islands (Days 2-5, in dayFeatures().fuel). Warnings at 25% and 10% (engine coughs), and at 0% the engine stops and the plane glides nose-down (advance input.engineOut). The invisible 70 m floor is gone in flight: dist/hazards.js gives sea, beach and hillside floors plus obstacles (Kestrel lighthouse, mail masts). Within 35 m of the floor while sinking a PULL UP warning flashes and beeps. A crash costs one parcel, 15% cargo condition and 15 s, then the plane respawns level at a safe spot from about five seconds earlier (out-of-fuel crashes come back with 35% fuel). No crashes all day earns a +100 clean-flight bonus.

Graphics (v0.54, dist/graphics.js): the sun follows the plane (high, behind-right) with soft shadow maps, so the plane always casts a shadow that helps judge height (shadows are off on touch devices); a gradient sky dome that follows the scene's background color (so Day 5's sunset and night carry through) with a sun glow; the ocean is a finer faceted sphere with gentle animated swell and vertex-colored lagoons and shallows around every island, plus pulsing shoreline surf; islands gain palms, rocks, bushes, flower beds and a dock with a dinghy (separate random seed; kept off pads, runway, apron and lighthouse); fuller two-tone clouds; plane cowling, exhaust stubs and puffs, wingtip navigation lights, a tail beacon, the pilot's goggles and flapping scarf and a propeller blur disc; water splashes for parcels and crashes in the sea; a soft vignette.

On the water (v0.54, dist/sealife.js, tests/sealife.test.mjs): twelve ships on looping routes (sailboats, tugs, container ships with cranes, trawlers) with wakes, funnel smoke and horns when you pass; four whales that surface, blow and dive; three dolphin pods; fourteen rocky sea stacks (some with beacons). Days 3-5 add six wandering waterspouts with storm clouds: within about 100 m they shake the controls and wear cargo condition, and their core is a crash. Ship masts, sea stacks and waterspout cores are crash obstacles. All positions were generated once and are tested to stay clear of islands, pads, fuel hoops, mail masts and the landing approach.

Bigger world (v0.58): RADIUS in dist/flight.js went from 150 to 200 (everything is placed relative to it; tests use it too). The eight original islands are about 15% larger and further apart; three new islands fill the biggest stretches of open sea: Coral Keys and Orchid Isle (Day 5's fuel hoops) and Puffin Rocks (a Day 3 stop, the Puffin Rocks observatory, replacing the pearl diver hut). The home island keeps its original distances around the runway (Kestrel positions scaled by 0.75), so the runway, apron, lighthouse, depot and Day 1 stops feel the same. Retuned for the longer flights: par times x1.3 (390/470/550/700/860 s), urgent clocks 125 s and 560 s, fuel burn about 25% lower (a tank lasts about six minutes at cruise), the route map shows the same ~100 units around you, more water glints and clouds. Ship routes, sea stacks, waterspouts and sea life were regenerated for the new layout (16 ships, 18 stacks) and are re-checked by tests/sealife.test.mjs. Islands are now built from the shared ISLANDS list in days.js.

Endless run (v0.62, the main mode; dist/run.js, tests/run.test.mjs): one continuous game made of days. Each day the crew loads the plane (cutscene), the clock runs from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM over the day's length, and you must deliver the day's quota and land through the gold gate before night. The run ends at nightfall, or when the tank runs dry and the plane goes down (training stays forgiving with the tow crew). Day N: quota min(7, N+1); islands unlock outward from home (1, 2, 4, 6 ... all 11); stops are a random cluster from the 28-stop pool (every training stop plus the two boats); the clock is the estimated route time x slack (2.18 on Day 1 down to 1.32), so days get tighter. Hazards arrive on a schedule: Day 2 wind (stronger each day up to 6), Day 3 an urgent parcel and gulls, Day 4 a boat stop and waterspouts, Day 5 storms and mailbags, Day 7 two boats, two urgent parcels and three mailbags. Fuel hoops thin out: all six on Days 1-2, one fewer each day, a single hoop from Day 7; when a day is longer than a tank, the hoop nearest the route is always kept. The tank is full each morning and the blue depot at home still refuels. Sunset and dusk warnings at 72% and 88% of the day. Day-complete card: today's points, daylight bonus (2 per second left), landing grade, no-crash bonus, and a preview of tomorrow. Best run (days and points) is saved per browser (special-delivery-best-run). The five hand-made days are now Training (tab on the title card), unchanged.

Runs without landings (v0.64): an endless run is one continuous flight. After the opening takeoff you never land: when a day's quota (and any mailbags) is done, the day ends in the air, a 4.5 s pause lets the last delivery play out, dawn breaks (a warm screen wash hides the sky change) and the next day is planned from the plane's current position (planDay(day, seed, from)). Fuel carries over between days; the only fuel is the green hoops (thinning out daily; the hoop nearest each day's route is always kept) and the blue depot at home. The crew air-drops fresh parcels at dawn (back to 12) and cargo condition resets. Daylight and no-crash bonuses are added at each dawn; the gold gate and landing are training-only now. R or Restart in a run restarts the whole run.
